# AI planner (arc 3) · slice 15: Eye design critique (LITE, review mode)

**Scope.** This reviews the arc-3 surfaces:
- the Plan tab: the form, the proposal card, and ghost blocks on the plan day's timeline (slice 13);
- Settings → Planning (slice 14);
- the four snag fixes carried in this arc (slices 3, 5, 7, 9).

Each is judged against the ratified FINAL design (`docs/design/2026-09-27-final/spec.md`, `src/styles/tokens.css`). It is a critique only. No new directions are proposed, and no ratified decision is re-opened. Code was read wherever a capture alone was ambiguous or a state was not captured: `Plan.tsx`, `PlanGhosts.tsx`, `plan.css`, `PlanningSettings.tsx`, `actions.ts`, `chrome/Header.tsx`, `Planner.tsx`, `editor/sheet.css`, `styles/app.css`, `components/Toast.tsx`, `data/repo.ts` (`resetAiProfile`).
**Accepted as intended (not findings):** sharp text under the floating tab bar in the Playwright-WebKit iPhone captures (A2-P0-3, a headless-compositing artefact). Also accepted: the Plan tab's own timeline shows the "now" line (08:00 fixture clock).
**Severity:** 🔴 P0 = must fix before the arc ships (broken, illegible, inaccessible or misleading, or an originality breach) · 🟡 P1 / ⚪ P2 = new GitHub issue labelled `snag` · 🟢 = fine. Size S = under half a day, M = around a day.

**Verdict.** The propose → review → accept loop is sound and well explained. Each block carries its "why". The notes callout says what was dropped. The ghosts are unmistakably proposals in both themes, and Auto has a real, exact Undo. One P0 remains: **the Plan page has two date pickers that disagree.** The app header (title, ‹ ›, week strip, stats) still drives the board's date, while Plan has its own Today / Tomorrow / date choice. Pick "Tomorrow" and the header still says "Saturday 3 Oct · 0h30 planned" above a timeline of Sunday, and Accept writes to the day the header isn't showing. The rest is P1/P2 polish:
- disabled buttons that look live;
- Accept → Undo losing the proposal (and still teaching the profile);
- Auto silently dropping the planner's notes and questions;
- free-gap chrome drawn through ghosts;
- the uncaptured states (not connected, offline, edit, clash).

## 🟢 Fine / praise

- **AI-G-1 The ghost language is right.** It is dashed, category-tinted, chip-less and rounded-rect, against the solid pill-with-chip of real blocks. Proposals never pass for commitments in either theme, and the "why" line rides inside the ghost once it is ≥ 56px tall.
- **AI-G-2 Explainability is first-class.** Each block has its own "why", a notes callout states what was dropped and why, and sources carry a title + snippet. This is the core of "AI that plans with you", and it reads calmly rather than chattily.
- **AI-G-3 The accessible surface is the list, not the ghosts.** Ghosts are `aria-hidden`. The list has 44pt ticks with `aria-pressed` + "Keep {title}", line-through and ink-2 for unticked items, labelled edit fields, and `aria-live` on the card.
- **AI-G-4 Undo is exact.** `undoPlan` tombstones only the tasks the plan produced (`accepted_task_ids`), and nothing else on the day.
- **AI-G-5 The mobile card lands in view.** `scrollIntoView` + `scroll-margin-top: --header-h-mobile` put "Before you accept" right under the week strip, with nothing hidden under the header.
- **AI-G-6 Settings → Planning is a good grouped card.** The h3 sits inside the card (the ST-P0-2 pattern), the learned profile is in plain words with its provenance ("From 1 accepted plan"), and the choices carry their consequence in the label.
- **AI-G-7 All four snag fixes landed:**
  - **ST-P1-2 brand mark:** the chip is now a darker disc *inside* the top pill with a white tick, and the pills have a gap. The toggle-knob reading is gone, and it matches the product's block (`slice-3-brand-mark.png`).
  - **ST-P2-3 handshake:** now two forearms in a clasp. It reads as a handshake at 22px+ and no longer as a bow tie (`slice-7-handshake.png`).
  - **ST-P2-1 now line:** it stops at the running pill's edge, and "Write the migration plan" is clean (`slice-5-running-pill-iphone-15.png`).
  - **ST-P2-5 month:** the title is on the 16pt gutter, and ‹ › have a hairline edge (`slice-9-month-iphone-15-light.png`).
- **AI-G-8 Originality holds.** The Plan glyph is the generic four-point sparkle, an industry-wide "assistant" convention drawn in-repo (`ui-plan`), not another planner's property. "Plan", "Propose", "Re-plan" and "Before you accept" are plain generic copy. Dashed proposal blocks beside a tick list is our own layout. **No originality concern.**

## Flow review (UX soundness, within Meadow)

| Path | Verdict | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Propose → tick/edit → Accept all / some | 🟡 | Sound order and controls. Two problems: the day is ambiguous (**AI-P0-1**), and "Accept 3 ticked" is a dead live-looking button when all are ticked (AI-P1-1). The inline editor (title / start / minutes, Done / Cancel) is a correct minimal edit, but it is uncaptured (AI-P1-7). |
| Accept → Undo | 🟡 | The tasks come back out exactly. The proposal does not come back, and the profile has already learned from it (AI-P1-2). |
| Reject | ⚪ | Instant and final (AI-P2-3). |
| Auto → Undo | 🟡 | The toast + exact Undo are good. Notes and questions are discarded, and there is no "Undo & review" (AI-P1-3). The Auto chip lacks a consequence line (AI-P2-8). |
| Not connected / offline | 🟡 | The banner copy is clear and "Try again" exists. The form looks live (AI-P1-6). Offline should still let Mark draft (AI-P1-6). It is uncaptured (AI-P1-7). |
| Questions → re-plan | ⚪ | It works, but it posts the form's day and mode rather than the proposal's, resets edits without saying so, and has no Enter-to-submit (AI-P2-4). |
| Errors | 🟢 | `role="alert"` line under the submit; the copy is human ("Couldn't reach the planner — try again in a moment."). |
| iPhone reachability | ⚪ | Everything is reachable and clears the floating bar (`--scroll-pad-bottom`). The actions sit only above the timeline (AI-P2-5). |

**One-line verdict:** ship after AI-P0-1, the single date source. The P1s are small, contained fixes that make Undo and Auto trustworthy, and they should go in this arc's snag train before the AI layer gets daily use.
