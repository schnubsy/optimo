The Eye · FULL review · Marlowe Holm, Creative Director · 2026-09-27

optimo — six directions for a fun, bright, graphical planner

Mode: FULL (six authored directions, Flow review, scored rubric, handoff for winner + runner-up). Surface: Claude Code sub-agent without Task/shell — directions were authored one at a time with the collision check run after each; the Mac dossier and design register were unreadable here, so the portable dossier-lite was read silently and the collision check ran against the 2026-09-26 LITE pack only.

The project, as read

optimo is Mark's own day, placed: every intention gets a slot on one timeline, unscheduled work waits in an inbox, and free time is a visible thing you can drop into — not empty grid. It is used in two rituals (plan the morning, reconcile the evening) and dozens of one-thumb moves between them, so the hero job is placing and re-placing blocks — create, drag, resize-by-edge (which must persist duration; it does not today), complete, and inbox→timeline on a phone. Arc 1 shipped on Switchboard, a dark instrument panel Mark now calls "very technical"; his ask is a fun, bright, graphical, sleek register in the spirit of Structured — colour-coded bubbles with a line between them that says "you're free here" — without any of Structured's assets. Stack: React 18 + TypeScript + Vite, CSS-variable tokens, light + dark, iPhone 390 and desktop 1280, Google Fonts only, in-repo 24px icons.

1 · Fahrplan

Grid — Atticus VanceSwiss / Internationallight defaultblock strategy 2 · outlined card on a coloured spine

Archivo 800 — Saturday 26   Space Grotesk 09:00–11:00   Archivo 400/600 for UI

Desktop 1280 — two-pane. Colour lives on a 5px spine at the column's left edge and a 1.5px card outline; text is always ink on white. The free-time connector is a 2px grey rule with a boxed tabular label; tangerine is the one signal (now line, Place, selected tab).

iPhone 390 — single pane, square FAB, tab underline.

Secondary — week view: seven strict columns, blocks as outlined cards with a 3px edge, dotted free-time rules with the free hours per day in the header.

Rationale

The Swiss answer to "fun" is proportion: a bold Archivo date, oversized tabular hour numerals in Space Grotesk, and eight fully saturated category colours used as thin structural lines, so the timetable reads like a railway departure board that happens to be joyful. Blocks stay white with ink text — the single most legible option at 5-minute granularity, because a 6px-tall card still shows a coloured spine and an outline. Free time is drawn honestly as a rule and a number, not a mood.

What it gives up is softness. There are no tints, no chips, no springs; the brightness comes from saturated lines on white and from the tangerine signal. It is the direction to choose if "sleek" outweighs "bubbly".

Originality statement

No icon bubbles, no spine circles (the spine is a flat bar that never carries a glyph), no progress fill, gap label is a number in a box rather than prompt copy, no pink. Differs from Apple's left-bar chip (bar sits outside the card on the column edge), from Notion Calendar's monochrome (eight saturated lines), from Akiflow (no command bar, no purple), from Sunsama (no kanban).

Flow review — Ingrid Falk

  • Create quick-add parse row shows chips inline; tap empty slot creates at that time. Addressed: the parse row needs a 44px "wrong? edit" target — added.
  • Drag outline lifts to 2px + tangerine, ghost shows start/end in the gutter. Grid layout snaps to 5 min — good.
  • Resize by edge a 1.5px outline gives no grab affordance. Addressed: on hover/selected, a 44px invisible band below the card plus a 24×4 ink handle; commit writes duration_min only (the persistence bug is a data-path fix, called out in the prompt).
  • Complete square check + strike + 55% opacity — three cues, not colour.
  • Mobile inbox→timeline cross-tab drag is impossible; Place opens a slot list of real free rows. Open trade-off: the inbox tab needs a count badge (added) so unplaced work isn't invisible.
  • A11y ink on white 16:1; category colours are lines only, never text; now line has a time flag. Reduced motion: no transforms to drop.

2 · Gelateria

Vogue — Coco BelmontEditorial / Fashion (pop-editorial)light defaultblock strategy 1 · tinted rounded block with corner icon chip

Libre Caslon Display — Saturday, 26 September   Karla 700 titles   Karla 400/500 UI, tabular figures

Desktop — sherbet-tinted 16px blocks with same-hue ink text, a 26px icon chip in the block's top-right corner, italic Caslon hour numerals in the gutter, and a dotted connector with an italic "1 h 15 free" between blocks. Mango is the app accent.

iPhone — round FAB, pill date strip, tab bar with inbox count.

Secondary — task editor sheet (28px radius): title, category swatches, duration chips, start, priority; the same tint/chip pairs the block uses.

Rationale

An ice-cream counter is bright, sophisticated and edible-looking at once, which is the exact register asked for. Every block is a scoop: a 90%-lightness tint with the same hue at 32% lightness for text (≈10:1), so the colour is unmistakable and the words are always crisp. The editorial move is the type: Libre Caslon Display italic for the day title, hour numerals and the free-time label turns the timetable into a menu, and Karla keeps everything interactive plain and fast. The hero moment is the gutter itself — big italic hours you can read from across the room.

It trades a little density (16px radius and 12px padding cost about 20% vertical space versus Fahrplan) and puts a serif on screen, which some will read as "less app-like". The serif is limited to numerals, the day title and the gap label; no cream, no terracotta — the palette is white plus sherbet, so the warm-cream-serif tell does not apply.

Originality statement

Blocks are rounded rectangles with the icon inside them, not bubbles on a spine; there is no progress fill (running state = 2px ink outline + "now" flag); free time is a dotted connector with a duration, not a prompt sentence; the accent is mango, not pink; week view is compact tinted blocks, not icon stacks. Differs from Tiimo (no illustrated icon library, no ring/timer hero), Amie (no emoji identity, no bouncy springs, radius 16 not pill), Google Calendar (tints not solid fills, no tonal containers), Apple (chip top-right inside the block, no left bar).

Flow review — Ingrid Falk

  • Create quick-add is a pill field with a parse-preview row of chips; tap-empty-slot creates. Addressed: the italic gap label doubles as a create target (44px) — added.
  • Drag block lifts (scale 1.02, shadow), tint deepens one step, ghost keeps the chip; gutter shows the live start time.
  • Resize by edge radius 16 hides the edge. Addressed: selected block shows a 28×5 pill handle centred on the bottom edge, 44px hit band; live duration label while dragging; commit writes duration_min only.
  • Complete chip flips to a check glyph, title strikes, tint fades to neutral — shape + text + tone.
  • Mobile inbox→timeline Place button on every inbox row → slot sheet listing free connectors first. Open trade-off: below 25 min the chip hides and the block becomes a single-line pill; Flow accepts, with title truncation tested at 390px.
  • A11y tint/ink ≈10:1 light, ≈9.3:1 dark; chips with white text at L 0.55 (pistachio uses ink text); now line is ink, not red. Reduced motion: lifts become tone shifts.

3 · Bauklotz

Steel — Viktor StahlBauhaus (adapted from Brutalist — stated)light defaultblock strategy · solid saturated geometric block, shape-coded

Anton — SAT 26 SEP   DM Mono 09:00–11:00   Lexend 400/600 UI (Lexend substitutes the kit's Chivo, ruled out by the LITE pack)

Desktop — solid primary-coloured rectangles, zero radius, white (or ink on yellow) text, a white geometric marker per category (square = work, circle = meetings, triangle = health, diamond = personal). Free time is a 3px black line with a yellow tab; the now marker is a red flag on a black rule.

iPhone — black tab rule, round yellow FAB (the one circle, deliberately).

Secondary — week view: solid blocks read as a poster; today's column is yellow-tinted; free time as dotted black rules.

Rationale

Steel's honesty survives the move from a dark control room to a Bauhaus kindergarten: every block is a solid coloured object with its true size, every gap is a drawn line, nothing is implied. Brightness comes from saturation on white and from black used as a colour. Categories are encoded twice — hue and a white geometric marker — so the day is legible in greyscale, which no other direction manages without a glyph library. Anton's condensed display makes the date a poster headline; DM Mono keeps the rail tabular (a mono for times is a project need at 5-minute granularity, stated per the banned-defaults rule).

It gives up softness on purpose. White text on solid fills forces every category to L ≤ 0.55 (yellow and grey take ink text), and a full-fill day can feel loud by 6pm; the dark theme keeps hues and drops the canvas to near-black, which reads calmer.

Originality statement

Rectangles, not bubbles; markers inside blocks, not on a spine; no progress fill; the gap line is black with a number tab, not a dashed prompt; no pink. Differs from Google Calendar (zero radius, geometric markers, no tonal containers), Motion (no hatch/dashed task-vs-event, no density tables), TickTick (no list-dot system, no blue), Duolingo-style brights (no 3D lip).

Flow review — Ingrid Falk

  • Create boxed quick-add, parse preview as black-outlined chips; tap the yellow gap tab to create in the gap.
  • Drag block gets a 3px black outline and offsets 2px; no scale (Bauhaus does not bounce). Ghost is a black outline at the target.
  • Resize by edge Addressed: a black 44px band with a 32×4 handle appears on the selected block; live "1:30 → 1:45" in the gutter.
  • Complete block turns to a white box with a black outline, marker becomes a check, title strikes.
  • Mobile inbox→timeline Place picks from real gaps; Flow flagged that solid fills make overlapping blocks (side-by-side) hard to separate — addressed with a 2px white gutter between overlap columns.
  • A11y white on L 0.55 fills ≥ 4.5:1 for red/blue/green/violet/orange; cyan at 0.55 is 4.3:1 → lowered to 0.52; yellow/grey use ink. Open: the all-solid canvas has no reduced-contrast mode; noted.

4 · Meadow

Calm — Anaya ShresthaOrganic / Calm-techlight defaultblock strategy 3 · stacked pill rows with elapsed fill

Nunito Sans 800 — Saturday 26   600 titles · 400 body · tabular-nums times — one family, five weights

Desktop — full-width pill rows (radius 22px, min height 32px) on a warm-white canvas; a 28px circular chip sits inside the pill's left end; the running pill shows a soft elapsed fill from the left; free time is a dotted stem in sage with a leaf-green label pill.

iPhone — the pills are the touch targets; FAB in moss green.

Secondary — editor sheet with the same pill vocabulary: duration chips, category dots, gentle Save.

Rationale

The closest to Mark's word "bubbles" without borrowing Structured's bubbles: each task is a soft pill you could pick up, the day reads as a stack of stones in a stream, and the free time between them is a stem you can drop into. The palette is a spring meadow at noon — sky, grass, buttercup, heather, lavender, apricot — at 94% lightness with matching 32%-lightness ink, so it is bright without glare. One family (Nunito Sans) in five weights keeps the type friendly and the bundle small; times use tabular figures.

The pill's known cost: below 25 minutes proportionality is lost (a 10-minute call and a 25-minute call are both 32px). Calm accepts this as the price of touchability and shows a small "+2" cluster when three or more short items collide.

Originality statement

Pills with the chip inside, not circles on a spine; elapsed fill is a left-to-right tone sweep on the pill, never a circular fill; the free-time stem is dotted with a duration pill, no prompt copy; accent is moss, not pink; week view is compact pills, not icon stacks. Differs from Amie (radius capped at 22px, not 999; no emoji; no springs), Tiimo (no illustrated icons, no timer ring), Things (there is a timeline; no draggable "+").

Flow review — Ingrid Falk

  • Create pill quick-add with parse preview; the stem label is a create target.
  • Drag pill lifts with a soft shadow, neighbours reflow with 250ms ease; the stem re-measures live.
  • Resize by edge pills have no visible edge. Addressed: selected pill grows a 6px-wide translucent bottom band with a 32×5 handle (44px hit); live duration in the pill; commit writes duration_min only.
  • Complete chip → check, title strikes, pill tone drops to neutral; no confetti — Calm declines the burst by design.
  • Mobile inbox→timeline Place lists stems (free gaps) that fit; Flow flagged the short-block cluster ("+2") needs a tap-to-expand list — added.
  • A11y tint/ink ≈10:1; running fill is tone, not hue; now line is moss with a time pill; reduced motion drops lifts and reflow, keeps tone.

5 · Prism

Neon — Mr. NeonGlassmorphism / Futurist (daylight glass — stated)light default (stated)block strategy 1 variant · tinted card with luminous gradient edge + chip

Syne 800 — SAT 26   Sora 600 titles · 400 UI · tabular-nums

Desktop — a cyan→violet daylight gradient canvas, frosted white cards (78% white, no blur on content), a 4px luminous gradient edge per block, gradient icon chips, and a glowing 2px thread as the free-time connector. Only the rail, top bar and tab bar use backdrop blur.

iPhone — glass tab bar over a plain canvas; violet FAB.

Secondary — editor sheet on glass; the day-capacity arc (Neon's luminous chart) lives in the sheet header as "8h15 open".

Rationale

Neon's job is the screenshot, and the screenshot here is a day that glows: a soft dual-tone gradient sits under everything, blocks float as frosted cards with a lit gradient edge, and the free-time thread literally lights up the gap. It is the most "graphical" direction and the one that feels most like 2026. Neon left his dark default because the brief says bright; the dark theme is the same system with a deep-indigo gradient and brighter edges.

The costs are real: contrast on a gradient canvas needs opaque cards (78–85% white) to hold 4.5:1, blur is limited to chrome per NN/g, and a gradient border per block costs a pseudo-element — buildable, but the heaviest of the six, and the look will date fastest.

Originality statement

Frosted cards with an inner chip, no spine circles; glow thread instead of dashed prompt; no progress fill (running = brighter edge + now thread); accent violet, not pink; no icon-stack week. Differs from Apple Liquid Glass (no glass over content, no floating toolbars over the timeline; blur only on the rail/bars with an opaque fallback), Fantastical (no rainbow parser field), Amie (no emoji, radius 14).

Flow review — Ingrid Falk

  • Create glass quick-add with a parse-preview strip; the thread label creates in the gap.
  • Drag card lifts with a glow; the target gap's thread brightens. Nice, and it stays transform-only.
  • Resize by edge Addressed: selected card shows a gradient 32×4 handle on the bottom edge with a 44px band; duration label glows while dragging; commit writes duration_min only.
  • Complete chip → check, edge goes grey, title strikes — three cues.
  • →Mobile inbox→timeline the inbox as a glass sheet over the timeline failed Flow's first pass (text-over-text). Fixed: inbox is a full-height tab; Place lists lit threads. Open: glass bars must fall back to opaque under prefers-reduced-transparency and prefers-contrast: more.
  • A11y ink on 78% white over the gradient ≈ 11:1 (verified at the darkest gradient corner); chips with white text at L 0.56–0.62 need the ink-text rule for lime/gold; glow pulses stop under reduced motion.

D · Recommendation — Marlowe Holm

Winner: Gelateria (Vogue · Editorial). It is the only direction that scores 8+ on every criterion Mark weighted, and it answers his sentence literally: colour-coded blocks you could pick up, a line between them that tells you how long you are free, in a register that is bright and grown-up at once. The tint/ink recipe makes it the safest of the "fun" three at 5-minute granularity and in dark mode, and it is a tokens-and-CSS change for the existing components — no new layout engine.

Runner-up: Meadow (Calm · Organic). If the serif numerals feel like a magazine rather than a planner after a week, Meadow is the same tint/ink system in a rounder, softer, single-family body — closest in spirit to the Structured feel Mark referenced, at the cost of proportionality below 25 minutes.

Named tension. Mark's reference (Structured) is rounded-sans; Gelateria's identity is a serif in the gutter. If he wants the reference's softness more than a distinct voice, pick Meadow. Optional hybrid: Gelateria's palette and blocks with Meadow's Nunito Sans in place of Caslon + Karla — lower risk, less memorable; the tokens make this a two-line swap.
