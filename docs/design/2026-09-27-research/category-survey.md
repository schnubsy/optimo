# Visual daily-planner category survey (2025–2026)

Purpose: learn the category's conventions, name each brand's signature so optimo avoids it. Method: web sources (below) for verifiable 2025–26 changes; block-level rendering details are from direct product observation up to mid-2026 and marked (obs.) where no citable source describes them. Structured.app is deliberately excluded from the per-app list but its signature (icon-in-circle on a vertical spine, dashed free-time gaps, candy tints) is the primary thing NOT to echo.

## Per-app notes

**Tiimo** (neurodivergent-first visual planner)
- Blocks: tall rounded cards (~12–16px radius) fully filled with a pastel tint; large activity icon at left, title + time; running activity gets a progress ring / countdown timer (obs.; "progress ring" confirmed on product page).
- Free time: blank canvas between cards, no explicit gap card. Now: timer-driven "current activity" card is the indicator more than a red line.
- Icons: thousands of illustrated, filled activity icons (yoga, cooking, laundry…) chosen per task; icons are the identity system. Palette: pastel lilac/cream/beige site, candy-pastel tints in app; dark mode = charcoal with the same pastel fills.
- Mobile: bottom tab bar + centre "+" button; bottom sheets for task edit.
- Do NOT copy: the illustrated activity-icon library + focus ring/timer as the hero of the day view.

**Sunsama** (guided daily planning)
- Blocks: calendar column at right, events as soft solid fills in per-calendar colour, ~6–8px radius, thin darker left edge on some sources (obs.). Tasks live as cards in day columns, not primarily on the timeline.
- Free time: plain grid. Now: thin red horizontal line with dot (standard). Icons: minimal outline set, channel favicons (Notion/Asana) on task cards.
- Palette: warm, calm, muted editorial (beige/cream light mode, warm-grey dark mode); serif-free, generous whitespace. Mobile: bottom tabs (Today / Calendar / Objectives).
- Do NOT copy: the multi-day kanban of task cards beside a calendar + guided morning/evening ritual screens ("Daily shutdown").

**Akiflow** (power-user command-bar planner)
- Blocks: rectangular, small radius (~6px), full solid fill for events, tasks show a checkbox inside the block; multiple calendars overlap side by side (obs.). Radius scale 6/10/16/pill; purple (#8B00FF-class) primary; "generous whitespace… subtle drop shadows" (OpenDesign).
- Free time: grid. Now: red line. Icons: outline monoline; integration favicons everywhere.
- Palette: white canvas, dark ink, single high-chroma purple accent; dark mode = near-black with same purple. Mobile: bottom tabs + command bar sheet.
- Do NOT copy: the command bar (⌘K) as the primary interaction plus the "Ritual" checklists and integration-favicon-heavy inbox.

**Morgen** (calendar + tasks, AI "Frames")
- Blocks: solid fills in muted per-calendar colours, ~4–6px radius, tasks scheduled as blocks with a checkbox; AI-suggested placements render as ghost/outlined blocks before confirming (obs.; Frames preview→adjust→schedule confirmed on site).
- Free time: grid; Frames tint background bands of the day (e.g., "deep work" band). Now: red line.
- Palette: cool, restrained, blue-grey SaaS neutral; dark mode = standard graphite. Icons: outline. Mobile: bottom tabs.
- Do NOT copy: Frames (named background bands guiding an AI planner) and the ghost-preview scheduling loop.

**Amie** (playful calendar + todos, pivoted to AI notes)
- Blocks: pill-ish cards with large radius (~10–14px), full pastel fill, emoji leading the title; todos as draggable chips that become "plannable blocks of time" (screensdesign). Springy motion on drag/resize; bottom nav bar changes contextually.
- Free time: grid. Now: line with a small pill time label. Icons: emoji as the icon system.
- Palette: candy pastels on white; dark mode = deep grey with the same saturated pastels. Mobile: adaptive bottom bar, big rounded sheets.
- Do NOT copy: emoji-first event identity, "notch" floating overlay UI, and the bouncy spring animation language.

**Routine** (calendar + notes + console)
- Blocks: solid fills, ~6px radius, calm per-calendar hues; tasks appear with a checkbox ring; "console" overlay (⌘J-style) for capture (obs.).
- Free time: grid. Now: red line. Icons: thin monoline; minimal, "gorgeous… minimalistic" (Efficient App).
- Palette: monochrome-leaning with one accent; dark mode = flat dark grey. Mobile: bottom tabs.
- Do NOT copy: the console overlay + notes-page-per-day pairing.

**Motion** (AI auto-scheduler)
- Blocks: dense, small-radius (~4px) solid fills; auto-scheduled tasks visually distinct from fixed events (lighter tint / hatch or dashed outline variants across versions) (obs.).
- Free time: grid. Now: red line. Icons: outline; heavy use of priority/status badges.
- Palette: enterprise SaaS (blue/grey), high density, tables and kanban next to the calendar; dark mode = standard.
- Do NOT copy: the constantly re-shuffling auto-schedule look and the "task vs event" hatch/dashed distinction as the main visual differentiator.

**Fantastical** (Flexibits)
- Blocks: solid calendar-colour fills with rounded ends, tighter radius; DayTicker strip of days at top; Liquid Glass toolbars and controls moved to the bottom on iPhone in 4.1 (Flexibits). "Rainbow glow" around the natural-language parser field when Apple Intelligence assists.
- Free time: grid. Now: red line. Icons: SF Symbols. Palette: red brand + calendar hues, restrained; dark mode = system.
- Do NOT copy: DayTicker, the natural-language parser bar with rainbow glow, red brand accent.

**Apple Calendar (iOS 18/26 Liquid Glass)**
- Blocks: per-calendar colour as a light tint fill with saturated left bar and coloured title text (iOS 18 style persists), small radius (~4–6px). Compact/Stacked/Details/List switch.
- Free time: grid. Now: red line + red time bubble in the gutter. Icons: SF Symbols. Navigation floats as translucent glass bars/pill tab bars over content (MacRumors); NN/G documents contrast loss when text/controls sit over content.
- Palette: system tints, white/black canvas; dark mode = system dynamic materials.
- Do NOT copy: glass/translucent floating toolbars over the timeline; the Apple left-bar-plus-tint event chip in its exact proportions.

**Google Calendar (Material 3 Expressive, Aug 2025)**
- Blocks: solid full-fill chips in calendar colour, ~8px radius, white text; hour/day slots now sit in containers with a solid primary-tint background replacing faint lines; month days separated into rounded cells "like a chocolate bar" (9to5Google).
- Free time: tinted container grid. Now: red line + dot. Icons: Material Symbols; FAB for create; bottom bar.
- Palette: Dynamic Color from wallpaper; dark mode = tonal dark surfaces. Monthly header illustrations.
- Do NOT copy: Dynamic-Color tonal containers, the chocolate-bar month grid, the FAB + month illustration combo.

**Things 3** (Cultured Code; list, not timeline)
- No time blocks; tasks are text rows with a large circle checkbox, blue/yellow/green/teal semantic list icons, "Today" star, "This Evening" moon section, headings as light separators.
- Palette: near-white with restrained accent colours per view; dark mode = graphite with the same accents. Whitespace and typography carry the design; no emoji.
- Mobile: bottom "+" magic button that can be dragged to drop a task into a list; tab-less, list-based navigation.
- Do NOT copy: the draggable "+" magic button, Today/Evening split, per-view icon colours.

**TickTick (timeline / calendar view)**
- Blocks: solid fills in list colour, ~4px radius, tasks show a checkbox inside; "Arrange tasks" side panel for dragging undated tasks onto the grid; timeline (Gantt-like) view for projects.
- Free time: grid. Now: red line. Icons: monoline + list colour dots. Palette: utility blue accent, neutral; many themes incl. dark.
- Do NOT copy: Arrange-tasks drawer + list-colour dot system; TickTick's blue.

**Notion Calendar** (ex-Cron)
- Blocks: solid per-calendar fills with subtle outline, ~6px radius, keyboard-first; auto/light/dark theme, zoomable hour density, 1–3 days on mobile (Notion Help). Notion pages linked into events show the page icon.
- Free time: fine grid; overlapping calendars stack with translucency. Now: red line. Icons: monoline, Notion doc icons. Palette: monochrome editorial with muted calendar hues; dark mode = pure dark grey.
- Do NOT copy: the multi-timezone rail, the "Cron" monochrome keyboard-first aesthetic, Notion page-icon embedding.

## Synthesis

### Generic conventions (safe to use)
1. Vertical hour gutter on the left, 24h or configurable range, labels every hour, faint lines/containers every 30 min.
2. Block height = duration; blocks are rounded rectangles with text top-left (title, then time).
3. Colour per category/calendar; the block's colour is the primary category encoding.
4. A "now" horizontal line spanning the day column with a time bubble in the gutter.
5. Drag to move, drag bottom edge to resize, snap to 5/15-minute grid; long-press on mobile.
6. Unscheduled tasks live in a side panel / inbox and drag onto the grid.
7. Completed blocks dim (reduced opacity, strikethrough or checkmark).
8. Overlapping items split the column width or stack with translucency.
9. A create affordance (+) fixed near the bottom on mobile; bottom sheet for editing.
10. Light/dark themes with system-follow; dark mode keeps hue, drops lightness.

### What reads "fun, bright, graphical" vs "technical"
- Radius: fun 12–20px cards (Tiimo/Amie); technical 2–6px (Motion/TickTick/Notion Calendar). 8–10px is the neutral middle.
- Colour: fun = pastels at ~85–92% lightness, 40–60% chroma, with a saturated icon/accent; technical = single accent + greys, calendar colours at full saturation on small chips.
- Fill: fun = full soft tint + dark text; technical = solid saturated fill + white text or hairline outline.
- Icons: fun = filled/duotone or illustrated glyphs 24–28px in a chip; technical = 16px monoline, or none.
- Whitespace: fun = 12–16px card padding, 4–6px gaps between blocks; technical = 4–8px padding, zero gaps.
- Typography: fun = rounded/geometric sans, 16–17px titles; technical = system sans at 13–14px.
- Motion: fun = springs, drag-lift scale 1.02–1.04, checkmark pops; technical = 150ms linear fades.
- Free time: fun apps make it visible (gap labels, "2h free"); technical apps leave it as grid.

### Three non-signature time-block strategies
1. **Tinted rounded block with corner icon chip** — 10px radius, category tint at ~90% L, 28px circular icon chip top-right inside the block, dark title text. Pros: strong colour + glyph redundancy, dark text passes contrast on all tints. Cons: below ~25 min (about 30px at 1.2px/min) the chip must hide; keep the touch target ≥44px by expanding the hit area beyond the visual block.
2. **Outlined card on a coloured spine** — 1.5px category-colour outline, white/dark surface fill, a 4px vertical category spine on the timeline's left edge (not inside the card, unlike Apple's left bar), title in ink. Pros: highest text legibility, good at 5-min granularity because a hairline card still reads at 6–8px tall; overlapping cards stay readable. Cons: less "graphical"; rely on the spine + a small dot for colour identity; the spine must not become a Structured-style icon spine (no circles on it).
3. **Stacked pill rows** — blocks as full-width pills (height = duration, min 32px, radius = half height capped at 12px) with a duration-proportional progress fill sweeping left→right as time elapses; icon left, title, end-time right. Pros: obviously touchable, fun without emoji, progress is visible without a ring. Cons: very short items collapse into a 32px pill and lose proportionality; needs an "n more" cluster for many 5-min items; pills should not use 999px radius (that's Amie/M3 territory).

### Accessibility notes
- White text on candy colours fails WCAG AA: e.g., white on #F9A8D4 pink ≈ 1.9:1, on #FDE68A yellow ≈ 1.3:1, on #86EFAC green ≈ 1.5:1. Use dark ink (#1F2937-class) on tints ≥80% L, or saturate to ≤50% L before using white text. Test every category colour in both themes.
- Colour-only encoding fails 1.4.1: pair each category with a glyph and/or a text label; give "task vs event" and "done" a shape/opacity difference, not just hue.
- Do not rely on tint alone in dark mode; tints on dark surfaces often collapse to 1.5–2:1 vs neighbours. Add a 1px border at ~20% of the category colour.
- Now-line: red-only is problematic for deuteranopia; add a time label and a distinct thickness.
- Avoid translucent/glass chrome over the timeline (NN/G on Liquid Glass); keep 44×44 px minimum targets and ≥8px spacing between adjacent short blocks.
