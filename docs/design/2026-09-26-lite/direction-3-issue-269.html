Direction 3 — Issue 269

Seat: Vogue (Coco Belmont) · Movement: Editorial / Fashion · Default mode: light (the "night edition" is true black) · Type: Bodoni Moda + Work Sans

Every day is an issue. Today is Issue 269 of the year, and it opens the way a magazine does: a full-bleed cover photograph — the user's own, or a monochrome auto-cover — with the day's name set in Bodoni at cover size. Below the fold, the day is typeset, not boxed: hours are italic folio numerals in the gutter, each block is a typographic entry with a single coloured rule and no fill, the unplaced list is the issue's contents page, and the quick-add is a single italic line you dictate into. There is exactly one colour that means "act": lipstick red, on the now line, on Place, on Add, and on the strike that crosses a finished entry — which is the most satisfying gesture in the system. The categories are a couture swatch card (ink navy, bordeaux, camel, forest, plum) sampled as if from a fabric wall; they are identity, never alarm. Zero radius and no shadows because paper has neither; a grabbed entry lifts by outline and scale, and the day-to-day turn is a slow 420 ms crossfade. This direction spends its boldness on the masthead and keeps everything under it disciplined; the departure from the seat's usual hero is deliberate — a planner's daily cover is the natural full-bleed moment.

Flow review (Ingrid Falk) — within the aesthetic

  • 🟡 Create: an italic Bodoni placeholder reads as content, not an input. Addressed: the dictate line carries a 1.5 px rule beneath it, a red Add action, a visible caret on focus, and the parse reads back in prose ("reads as Coffee with Reza, tomorrow 9:00, 30 min") before commit.
  • 🟡 Drag-to-reschedule: fill-less entries offered nothing to grab. Addressed: grab cursor across the whole entry, grip dots appear on hover/hold, the lifted entry gets a 2 px ink outline and 2% scale, and the ghost is a red dashed band with the folio time in the gutter. Snaps to 5 min.
  • 🟡 Resize: the bottom rule was 1 px. Addressed: hover/selected thickens it to 3 px with a 12 px hit band (44 px on touch when selected) and a live italic duration.
  • 🟢 Complete: square check at 44 px; the red strike across the title is the direction's signature gesture. Undo toast for 5 s.
  • 🔴 → 🟡 Mobile inbox → timeline: the first pass relied purely on long-press drag out of the sheet, which is undiscoverable. Fixed: a red Place action on every unplaced entry (next open half hour, or pick a time); the sheet collapses to a strip during any drag so the day stays visible. Still needs a real-device test for the sheet-over-timeline gesture.
  • 🟡 Legibility: Bodoni's hairlines break below 16 px on mobile. Rule adopted: Bodoni never smaller than 16 px; everything small is Work Sans. Red on white is 5.9:1; red on black (night edition) is 5.6:1.
  • 🟡 Open trade-off: white space stands in for free time. Gaps are named in small italics ("an hour open") and become an Add target on hover/tap, but they are quieter than either rival direction — accepted by the author as the cost of the page feel.
