Direction 1 — Slack Water

Seat: Calm (Anaya Shrestha) · Movement: Organic / Calm-tech · Default mode: light · Type: Newsreader + Spline Sans

Slack water is the still point between tides — the moment the day stops pulling. The timeline is a soft channel, not a grid: blocks sit in it like river stones, hours are whispered in the gutter, and the one drawn thing on the screen is the daylight arc, which answers the only anxious question a planner gets asked ("how much day is left?") without a number. The palette is sampled from wet slate, sea glass and lichen — cool, low-chroma naturals, deliberately not cream, with a single sea-glass accent that carries every action. Spline Sans does the work; Newsreader speaks only when the app addresses you (the day, the section heads, the italic "1 h free"). Motion is slow-settle (240–320 ms, ease-out) and never decorative: a block lifts when grabbed and eases into its slot; nothing else moves on its own.

Flow review (Ingrid Falk) — within the aesthetic

  • 🟢 Create: persistent quick-add on both layouts; parsed chips preview before commit, Enter creates, chips are tappable to correct a bad parse.
  • 🟡 Drag-to-reschedule: soft blocks lacked a drop cue. Addressed: dashed ghost snapping to 5-minute grid plus a live time label in the gutter; the lifted block gets the one strong shadow in the system.
  • 🟡 Resize: no visible handle in the first pass. Addressed: bottom pill on hover/focus (desktop) and whenever a block is selected (mobile), 44 px hit area, live duration tag.
  • 🟢 Complete: leading circle, 44 px target, block fades to 55% and strikes through; undo via toast for 5 s.
  • 🟡 Mobile inbox → timeline: dragging out of a bottom sheet is fragile. Addressed: every unplaced item has a Place button (next free slot that fits, or pick a time); long-press drag is the secondary path, and the sheet collapses to a peek while dragging.
  • 🟡 Open trade-off: low-chroma palette. Ink-2 on the mist background is 4.8:1; category tints never carry meaning alone (icon plus label always present). Priority "High" is text, not colour.
  • 🟢 Keyboard/focus: every block is a button; arrow keys nudge by 5 min, Shift+arrow by 15; visible 2 px accent focus ring.
